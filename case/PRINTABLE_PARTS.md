# Current printable enclosure

The release uses these user-supplied STL files:

| File | Supplied revision | Measured outer bounds in millimetres |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `printable/CamBuildCase.stl` | V1.3 | 212.8 × 96.2 × 34.0 |
| `printable/CamBuildFront.stl` | V1.3 | 174.6 × 76.75 × 21.0 |
| `printable/CamBuildLid.stl` | V1.2 | 172.8 × 85.55 × 22.0 |

The six-page `assembly/CamBuild_Assembly_Instructions_Updated.pdf` shows the Front on one side of the Case and the Lid on the opposite side. It labels two Front fasteners F1–F2 and three Lid fasteners L1–L3. Use five M3 screws and five standard M3 hex nuts, with each nut on the cavity side of a Case ear. Joint stack and screw length are described in the guide.

The STL files contain printable meshes, not editable Fusion feature history. A binary STL structure and dimensions check passed; mating fit, screw length, strap strength and hardware clearance have not been confirmed on the printed assembly. The older editable Fusion files and M4 design notes in this working folder are historical and are not included in the release.
